# Ting 0.7.5

## New

- **A converter in launcha.** Next to the calculator, launcha converts units
  (`5 yd to m`, `100 f in c`, `60 mph to km/h`), number bases (`255 to hex`,
  `0xff`), Unix time (`1700000000 to date`, `now to unix`) and dates
  (`now + 2 days`, `3 days ago`, `2026-12-25 - today`). The value comes first and
  `Return` copies it. Ting converts with the units and calendar of macOS; US and
  imperial units use their exact legal factors. Turn it off with **Convert in
  launcha** in Settings › General, or with `"converter": false` under `launcha`.

## Upgrade

A downloaded copy: choose **Check for Updates…** in Ting. A Homebrew copy:
choose **Update Ting via Homebrew…** in Ting's menu. Settings, bindings,
micro-apps and permissions are preserved. Requires macOS 14 or later; the
signed, notarized and stapled universal app supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
