# Ting 0.7.1

## Fixes

- The amber top rule follows the rounded outline of the launcha, leader and
  micro-app panels instead of extending beyond their upper corners.
- Up, Down, Page Up and Page Down navigate launcha results even when keyboard
  focus leaves the search field. Text composition and modified keys retain
  their editing behavior.
- Refreshing or reordering launcha results keeps the selected result in view
  instead of scrolling back to the first row.
- Command-arrow and Command-delete shortcuts reach the text editor in
  micro-app panels, including combinations with Shift and Option.

## Upgrade and install

Requires macOS 14 or later. The universal ZIP supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
Choose **Check for Updates…** in Ting to upgrade. Settings, bindings, micro-apps
and permissions are preserved.

For a manual installation, download `Ting-0.7.1-universal.zip` from
[Releases](https://github.com/bobuk-io/ting/releases/tag/v0.7.1), quit Ting,
replace `/Applications/Ting.app` with the extracted app, and open it.
The release is signed with Developer ID, notarized by Apple and stapled.
