# Ting 0.7.3

## New

- A small gear in the bottom-right corner of selecta and launcha opens the same
  menu as Ting's menu-bar icon, including Settings, updates and pause.
- The launcher closes before the menu opens so its keyboard handling does not
  interfere with menu navigation. The gear also works when the menu-bar icon is hidden.

## Upgrade

Choose **Check for Updates…** in Ting. Settings, bindings, micro-apps and
permissions are preserved. Requires macOS 14 or later; the signed, notarized
and stapled universal app supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
