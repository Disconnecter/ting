# Ting 0.7.19

- **Color themes across selecta, launcha, Clipbawd, and Settings:** **Settings › General › Appearance** now offers **System**, **Light**, and **Dark** modes plus **18 built-in palettes** (Amber, Sage, Ocean, Terracotta, Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Kanagawa, Everforest, Rosé Pine, Flexoki, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox, Solarized, One Light / One Dark, Ayu, Monokai, and GitHub) shared via BobukKit 0.5.0.
- **Full 10-color customization:** customize **Panel**, **Accent**, and **Text**, or expand **More colors** for **Surface**, **Muted text**, **Border**, **Selection**, **Link**, **Success**, and **Danger** with per-role **Auto** contrast reset, palette swatches, live preview, and WCAG contrast warnings. Selected rows in pickers use the theme's `selection` token, and palettes persist in `~/.config/ting/config.json`.
- **Shared launcher footer gear:** the bottom-right settings gear in selecta and launcha now uses BobukKit's shared footer control.

Settings, bindings, micro-apps, and history are compatible with 0.7.18.
