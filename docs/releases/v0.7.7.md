# Ting 0.7.7

## Fixed

- New bindings in Settings › Bindings now start with no keys. Ting no longer
  assigns the first free key (usually A) before you record a sequence.
- A new binding is saved only after you enter its keys and click **Done**.
  **Cancel** or Escape discards the draft without adding a binding.
- Editing an existing binding still starts with its current sequence.

## Upgrade

A downloaded copy: choose **Check for Updates…** in Ting. A Homebrew copy:
choose **Update Ting via Homebrew…** in Ting's menu. Settings, bindings,
micro-apps and permissions are preserved. Requires macOS 14 or later; the
signed, notarized and stapled universal app supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
