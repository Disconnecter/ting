# Ting 0.7.26

- **My Schedule:** a read-only calendar panel. Search for **My Schedule** in launcha, choose it from Ting's menu, or bind it in selecta (`"open": "schedule"`). It shows whether you are free now, your upcoming events and the useful free gaps of the next seven days. Up and down select an event; `Return` or a double-click joins its Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Jitsi, Whereby, Slack huddle or other recognized meeting link. Choose which calendars it reads in **Settings › My Schedule** (`schedule.calendars` in `config.json`; `null`, the default, means all of them). Contributed by Serhiy Zabolotnyy (#33).
- **Calendar access:** My Schedule asks for macOS Calendar access only when you click **Allow Calendar Access**. Ting only reads events: it never creates, changes or deletes them, keeps nothing about them on disk, looks at event notes only to find a known meeting link, and opens a link only when you choose the event.
- **Contributors:** About Ting now lists everyone who contributed to Ting on GitHub, each linked to their profile; the same list is in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

Settings, bindings, micro-apps, and history are compatible with 0.7.25.
