# Ting 0.7.6

## New

- **clipboarda, a clipboard history.** Open it with its own hotkey (Settings ›
  clipboarda), a selecta binding (`"open": "clipboarda"`) or **Open clipboarda**
  in Ting's menu. Search on top, the items of this session on the left, newest
  first, and a preview of the selected item on the right: the text, the image,
  the files or the color, with its type, source app, length or size, image
  dimensions and format, and when it was copied. `Return` pastes the item into
  the app in front, `⌘Return` copies it, `⇧⌘⌫` removes it. Search also
  understands text typed with the wrong keyboard layout.
- Copying the same thing again moves it to the top and counts it (`×3`) instead
  of adding it twice. When an app puts a picture of the copied text next to it,
  as Word does, clipboarda keeps the text.
- **This session only.** The history stays in memory, up to 200 items by
  default, and is gone when Ting quits.
- **Off if you do not want it.** Turn off **Use clipboarda** in Settings ›
  clipboarda (`"enabled": false`): Ting stops reading the clipboard, forgets the
  history, frees the hotkey and removes the menu item.
- **What is never kept:** copies while Secure Keyboard Entry is on, copies that
  password managers mark as secret, copies from Firefox private windows and,
  after you allow it, Chrome, Brave, Edge and Vivaldi incognito windows. Add apps
  to **Never keep copies from** with **Never Keep from…** in clipboarda or in
  Settings, and list text starts such as `sk-` or `ghp_` under **Never keep text
  that starts with**. Safari does not tell other apps about its private windows.
- **Link previews, off by default.** With **Fetch link titles, icons and images**
  on, a copied link shows its page title and favicon, or the image and its size
  for an image link. Ting sends one plain GET without cookies or scripts and
  never loads local network addresses.
- **Window bindings.** A selecta binding can open launcha or clipboarda:
  `{"key": "v", "open": "clipboarda"}`. Add one from **+** in Settings ›
  Bindings.

## Upgrade

A downloaded copy: choose **Check for Updates…** in Ting. A Homebrew copy:
choose **Update Ting via Homebrew…** in Ting's menu. Settings, bindings,
micro-apps and permissions are preserved. Requires macOS 14 or later; the
signed, notarized and stapled universal app supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
